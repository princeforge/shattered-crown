## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Defend your stronghold against relentless enemy waves in Hero Defense: Shattered Crown - a fantasy tower defense game where you command powerful heroes to protect your bases from destruction.

🛡️ DEFEND YOUR BASES
Build and upgrade your fortress with cannons, shields, and regenerative defenses. Three bases stand between you and defeat - lose them all and the battle is over. Choose your base type from 5 elemental themes: Fire, Water, Earth, Electric, and Nature.

⚔️ COMMAND UNIQUE HEROES
Unlock 15+ heroes, each with distinct abilities, spells, and elemental affinities. From the fire-wielding Cinderlord to the storm-summoning Tempest, every hero plays differently. Position them strategically around your bases to maximize their effectiveness.

🔥 DEEP STRATEGY
- 30 challenging levels across 10 biomes (Grassland, Desert, Snow, Volcanic, Forest, Ocean, Mountain, Swamp, Sky, Underworld)
- Level up your heroes mid-battle with powerful upgrades
- Cast devastating spells at crucial moments
- Synergy bonuses when heroes fight near each other
- Boss battles every 5 levels with unique mechanics
- Per-level challenges for bonus rewards (no hero deaths, speedruns, and more)

💎 PROGRESSION SYSTEM
- Earn crystals and essence from victories
- Unlock new heroes with crystals and stars
- Permanent hero progression - heroes level up across playthroughs (up to level 30)
- Fortune Shrine: invest essence to boost your luck stat
- Crystal Forge: spend crystals on permanent meta-upgrades
- Talent trees for each hero

🎮 GAMEPLAY FEATURES
- Touch-optimized controls: tap to select, tap to move, pinch to zoom
- Adaptive difficulty - the game adjusts to keep you challenged
- Daily login rewards and comeback bonuses
- Pre-game buffs to customize your strategy
- Shop system with rerolls for tactical decisions
- Beautiful procedural sprites and particle effects

🎯 PERFECT FOR FANS OF
- Tower defense games
- Hero-based strategy games
- Fantasy battle simulators
- Games with deep progression systems

No internet connection required to play. Progress saves automatically to your device.

## Play Console: Data Safety Form Answers

Fill these out in Play Console → App content → Data safety.

### Data collected (by the app directly)
- **None.** All game data is stored locally on the device only.

### Data collected by third-party services (must declare)

| Data type | Collected by | Purpose | Encrypted in transit | Can be deleted |
|-----------|-------------|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| App activity (app interactions, ad interactions) | Google AdMob | Ad delivery, analytics | Yes (HTTPS) | Reset ad ID in Android settings |
| Device or other IDs (advertising ID) | Google AdMob | Ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention | Yes | Reset ad ID in Android settings |
| Purchase history | Google Play Billing | Purchase verification & restore | Yes (managed by Google Play) | Via Google account settings |
| App activity (app interactions, in-game events) | Firebase Analytics | Anonymous usage analytics, game balance, retention | Yes (HTTPS) | Reset app data / uninstall |
| Crash logs, diagnostics, device info | Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reporting and debugging | Yes (HTTPS) | Reset app data / uninstall |
| Device or other IDs (app instance ID, Crashlytics UUID) | Firebase | Analytics and crash attribution | Yes (HTTPS) | Reset app data / uninstall |

### Data NOT collected
- Location (no GPS, no precise location - AdMob may use coarse country-level)
- Personal info (no name, email, account - unless contacting support)
- Photos, videos, audio, files
- Health & fitness
- Calendar, contacts
- SMS, phone

### Data handling
- **Encrypted in transit**: Yes (all third-party connections use HTTPS)
- **Can users request data deletion?** Yes - local data can be cleared via in-game "Clear Save" or uninstalling. Ad data: reset advertising ID in Android settings.

### Ads declaration
- **Yes, this app contains ads** - rewarded video ads via Google AdMob
